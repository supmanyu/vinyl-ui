## Usage

<Tabs items={['Web', 'React Native']}>

<FilmBurn className="rounded-md bg-ultraviolet p-6">Summer '86</FilmBurn>

## How it works

- **Web:** two radial gradients in `--color-burn` on a `::after` layer with `mix-blend-mode: screen`,
  drifting over `--film-burn-duration` (6s). The container gets `overflow: hidden` and `isolation: isolate`.
- **Native:** an `react-native-svg` radial-gradient overlay with `mixBlendMode: 'screen'`
  (New Architecture), animated by Reanimated.

Works best on mid-to-dark surfaces. On cream backgrounds the screen blend is barely visible.
